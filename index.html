The Katapayadi code of the 72 Melakarta ragas

Every raga hides its number in its name

Over 300 years ago, musicologists arranged the 72 parent (Melakarta) ragas of Carnatic music in a mathematical order. Then they named each raga so that its first two syllables encode its number, using the ancient Katapayadi system. Type a raga below and watch the algorithm decode it.
